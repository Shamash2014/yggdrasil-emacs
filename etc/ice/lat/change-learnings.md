# Learnings

Lessons from this change: owner feedback on a checkpoint, and review findings that recurred. A lead pastes the lines that apply into every later brief's CONTEXT.

Each line reads: date, source, lesson, where it applies, separated by " | ". The source is "owner N" for feedback on checkpoint N, or "review RN xK" for a finding that recurred K times. Where is a list of paths or globs, a feature name, all, or "this change". At archive, ice-archive-to-lat moves every line to lat.md/learnings.md, the source rewritten to "owner CHANGE#N", except those whose Where is "this change".
