# Learnings

Lessons from owner feedback and from review findings that recurred, one line each. A lead pastes the lines that apply into every brief's CONTEXT.

Each line reads: date, source, lesson, where it applies, separated by " | ". The source is "owner CHANGE#N" for owner feedback on a checkpoint, or "review RN xK" for a finding that recurred K times. Where is a list of paths or globs, a feature name, or all. When a line becomes a rule or a check, it ends with " | graduated: RN" or " | graduated: check PATH" and stays, so the history is kept.

## Lines

Newest last. ice-archive-to-lat moves lines here from a change's learnings.md when it is archived, all but those whose Where is "this change".
