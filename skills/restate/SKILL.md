# Restate

Say back, in your own words, what you think the owner's goals are and what
problem they are trying to solve. Do not copy their phrasing: a restatement
that repeats the ask proves nothing.

## Answer in this shape

Goals: what they want to be true when this is done, one line each.

Problem: what is wrong or missing today, and for whom.

Not the goal: anything nearby you think they do not want, when it is easy
to confuse.

Unsure: each point you had to guess, as a question with your guess as the
default.

## Rules

- Keep it short: a few lines per heading.
- Read only what you need to understand the ask. Change no files and start
  no work before the owner answers.
- End on the restatement and stop. The owner confirms or corrects it; a
  correction replaces your version, it is not a debate.
